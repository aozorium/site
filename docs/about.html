Dr. Olivia (Chen) Toyama

Representative Director ／ 代表理事
Associate Professor, Kyushu University
九州大学 准教授
Superconducting electronics ／ 超伝導エレクトロニクス研究
Read bio ／ 経歴を見る

Dr. Olivia (Chen) Toyama is an Associate Professor at Kyushu University, specializing in superconducting electronics research. Her work focuses on developing novel superconducting devices and circuits for quantum computing applications.

She has published numerous papers in top-tier journals and has been actively involved in international research collaborations. Dr. Toyama is committed to advancing the field of superconducting electronics and fostering the next generation of researchers.

遠山 オリビア（チェン）は九州大学准教授で、超伝導エレクトロニクス研究を専門としています。量子コンピューティング応用のための新しい超伝導デバイスと回路の開発に焦点を当てています。

トップジャーナルに多数の論文を発表し、国際的な研究協力に積極的に関わっています。超伝導エレクトロニクス分野の発展と次世代の研究者育成に取り組んでいます。

Dr. Christopher Ayala

Director ／ 理事
Head of Cryoelectronics, Atlantic Quantum
Visiting Professor, Yokohama National University
横浜国立大学 客員教授
Quantum computing ／ 量子コンピューティング研究
Read bio ／ 経歴を見る

Christopher Ayala (S'07-M'13-SM'20) received the combined B.Eng./M.Sc. degree in 2009, and completed his Ph.D. in 2012 from Stony Brook University, New York, USA in Electrical and Computer Engineering. From 2013 to 2015, he was a Post-Doctoral Fellow at IBM Research – Zurich, Switzerland.

From 2015, he was with the Institute of Advanced Sciences, Yokohama National University, Japan where he was an associate professor. Since 2024, he joined Atlantic Quantum where he is now the Head of Cryoelectronics while holding a Visiting Professor appointment at Yokohama National University.

His research interests include: quantum computing, emerging circuit technologies, superconductor logic, NEMS-MEMS, transformational compute, and electronic design automation.

Dr. Ayala is a member of The Japan Society of Applied Physics (JSAP); Institute of Electronics, Information and Communication Engineers of Japan (IEICE); Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE); Eta Kappa Nu Electrical and Computer Engineering Honor Society; and Tau Beta Pi Engineering Honor Society.

Activities

Our business activities事業内容

Academic events & conferences学術イベント

Planning and managing academic conferences, symposia, and workshops to facilitate knowledge exchange and collaboration among researchers.

学術会議、シンポジウム、ワークショップ等の企画・運営を通じて、研究者間の知識交換と協力を促進します。

Research dissemination研究成果の公開

Publishing and promoting research findings to make them accessible to the broader academic community and the public.

研究成果の公開および普及活動を行い、より広い学術コミュニティと一般社会への情報発信を目指します。

Education & training教育・人材育成

Providing comprehensive educational programs including training sessions, publications, and online courses to nurture future researchers.

研修、出版、オンラインコースを含む教育・人材育成に関する事業を展開し、次世代の研究者育成を支援します。

Resource management資金管理

Managing donations and grants from corporations and organizations, ensuring their appropriate allocation and utilization.

企業・団体からの寄付・助成金の管理および適切な運用を行い、研究活動の持続的な発展を支援します。

International collaboration国際交流

Promoting academic projects and international academic exchange to foster global research collaboration.

学術関連プロジェクトの推進および国際学術交流の促進を通じて、グローバルな研究協力を実現します。

Academic events support学術イベント支援

Organizing and supporting academic events and lectures to create platforms for knowledge sharing and discussion.

学術イベントや講演会の開催・支援を通じて、知識共有と議論の場を創出します。
